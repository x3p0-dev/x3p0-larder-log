Two steppers in 358px: 173 each, two 44px buttons and an 85px numeral cell. Playfair 700 at 26px here rather than the desktop 28, which holds four digits with 6.6px to spare. Four is the ceiling — a fifth does not fit, and the field should refuse it rather than let it collide. Chips go to 44px; the number and unit fields to 46.
09Keeping an item off the list
Keeping an item off the shopping list

Keep off the shopping list
It still shows as low or out on its card — it just never joins the list.
Last row of Count, under the two steppers — not a section of its own. Low-at says put this on the list when I am down to N; this says … except don’t. It modifies the threshold, so it lives where the threshold is set. The 22px box is the shopping list’s own checkbox at its own size, inverting exactly as every selected control in the app does.
