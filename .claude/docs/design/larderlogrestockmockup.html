The trip bar — anatomy

Four states, the undo toast that clearing earns, and where the green completion note goes instead.

The trip bar — anatomy
Below the grid, 24px down, full width of the content column. Trip management groups left; the trip's one action sits right. Hide and Clear checks are both ghosts and both about the ticks; the only filled control on the bar is the one that writes.
Nothing checked — there is no bar
Unchanged. The bar appears with the first check.
Some checked
Everything checked — the same bar, bigger numbers
Clearing earns an undo
Cleared 3 checks.
Under restock a check is worth something — it is a claim other people can see, and re-ticking a shop's worth of rows is not one tap. So clearing takes the actionable toast that already exists, and releases only your claims.
Where the green belongs — the whole list put away
Everything's put away.
Seven counts updated. Nothing is low or out.
Every row is put away to a count that clears its threshold, so a finished trip empties the list by arithmetic. This is where the 70px completion note's green disc goes — on the bar it was claiming nothing was pending while the counts still were.

The date is trustworthy and the quantity is not. Put-away doubles as drift correction, so new minus old is sometimes a purchase and sometimes a fix, and nothing can tell them apart. So tier 2 promises intervals, not rates — and stops short of you go through four a month, which is the sentence the data can't support.
