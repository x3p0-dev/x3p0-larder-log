The row that lets you drop one filter or all of them without opening the drawer. Everything below is live — point at a chip, tab into one, press it. Reset restores the set.

Clear filters is pinned and the chips scroll past the right edge — no gutter on that side, which is what says there is more. It costs about 110px of a 390 screen permanently, so roughly one and a half chips are visible at rest.

The menu button carries the crimson total so the fact that something is filtering survives scrolling past this row.
